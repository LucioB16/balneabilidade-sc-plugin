These terms govern the use of the independent Balneabilidade SC read-only MCP service, maintained through the LucioB16 GitHub repository.

1. What the service provides

The tools return already published IMA/SC bathing-water classifications, sample locations, dated measurements, histories and images of public bulletins for monitored beaches in Santa Catarina, Brazil. They do not provide real-time readings, forecasts, personal safety guidance or coverage outside their data source.

2. Independence and attribution

This is an independent project. It is not developed, operated, officially approved or endorsed by the Instituto do Meio Ambiente de Santa Catarina (IMA/SC). The original public data belongs to its respective source; see IMA/SC's official portal for the authoritative publications.

3. Accuracy and responsible use

Results reflect specific sample dates and sampling points, not current conditions at every location along a beach. Data can be delayed, incomplete or superseded, and the service can be unavailable. Before making a bathing decision, check the latest official bulletin, location-specific notices and on-site warnings. The service is informational and does not guarantee the safety of entering the water.

4. Appropriate use

Use the service for lawful, reasonable queries relevant to published bathing-water information. Do not attempt to overload it, bypass access or rate limits, interfere with IMA/SC systems or send credentials or sensitive personal data. We may limit access to protect service availability and the official data source.

5. Availability and changes

The service is currently offered without an account or payment. Availability, hosting and supported features may change or stop without a service-level guarantee. No purchases, subscriptions, booking or payment functions are provided by the MCP tools.

6. Open source and third parties
